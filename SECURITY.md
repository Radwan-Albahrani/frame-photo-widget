# Security

Frame has no network code, accounts or servers. Everything stays on your device, so most of the
usual attack surface does not exist.

If you do find a vulnerability, such as a way for another app to read Frame's photos or App Group
container, report it privately through
[GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Radwan-Albahrani/frame-photo-widget/security/advisories/new)
instead of opening a public issue. Include the steps to reproduce it and the iOS version you used.
